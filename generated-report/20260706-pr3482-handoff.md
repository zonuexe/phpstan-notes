## What the PR does

Implements the `@pure-unless-callable-is-impure $param` PHPDoc tag: a function/method/constructor
is pure unless the callable passed to `$param` is impure. Used for callback-accepting builtins
(array_map/array_filter/array_reduce/array_find/array_any/array_all/the array_u* comparator family/
call_user_func*/forward_static_call*/preg_replace_callback) and userland functions/methods.

Closes phpstan/phpstan#11100 and #11101. phpdoc-parser support: phpstan/phpdoc-parser#253 (merged).

## Key architecture facts (verified this session)

- Two levels: (1) function/method-level `getPureUnlessCallableIsImpureParameters(): array` map, and
  (2) parameter-level `ExtendedParameterReflection::isPureUnlessCallableIsImpureParameter(): bool`.
  Consumption reads the PARAMETER-level flag; the method-level getter is essentially interface-only.
- Consumption for calls: `SimpleImpurePoint::createFromVariant()` ->
  `resolvePureUnlessCallableIsImpureVerdict()` (now `public static`): iterates flagged params, matches
  args, AND-combines each arg's callable-acceptor `isPure()`. Yes=all pure, No=impure, Maybe=unknown,
  null=no flagged params. Verdict Yes -> no impure point; No -> certain; Maybe -> possibly.
- Constructors: `NewHandler::processConstructorReflection()` applies the verdict directly (NOT via
  createFromVariant, whose void-return heuristic forces certain=true and skips the verdict for
  void-returning constructors). Structured to mirror createFromVariant's yes()/no() branches.
- Stub-reflected generic builtins (array_find/array_find_key) go through `PhpFunctionReflection`
  (BetterReflection), which only read the PHPDoc tag; fixed BetterReflectionProvider::getCustomFunction
  to also merge functionMetadata.php.
- Method support was entirely non-functional until fixed: PhpClassReflectionExtension::createMethod
  now reads the tag from resolvedPhpDoc; PhpMethodReflection::getParameters now threads the flag.
- Two paths intentionally NOT threaded (documented with comments): createNativeMethodVariant (no
  built-in method carries the metadata) and Closure::call() params (closures cannot carry the tag).
- `@pure-callable $x` alone is NOT a tag; `@param pure-callable $x` (bare, resolves to
  `pure-callable(): mixed`) works and is treated as pure by the verdict.
